# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making EnterpriseERP Platform a respectful, professional and collaborative project.

Everyone participating in the project is expected to act with integrity, patience and respect.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Explain technical decisions clearly.
- Protect user, company and customer data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats or discriminatory language.
- Public sharing of private data, credentials or security details.
- Personal attacks or hostile communication.
- Deliberately introducing insecure or destructive code.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that violates this code of conduct, report it to the project maintainer.

Reports should include:

- What happened.
- Where it happened.
- Who was involved.
- Any relevant links or screenshots.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, restrict access or take other action needed to protect the project and its users.
